    Cat Orbs: Conquerors of Cat Earth

    Prowl the city, gobble treats and absorb every cat smaller than you. Only one orb can conquer Cat Earth.

    How to play
    • Steer with the mouse or WASD / arrows. Space to dash
    • Drag anywhere with your thumb to steer (a joystick appears). Lift your thumb and your cat keeps cruising the same way, so you can tap Dash any time. A quick tap without dragging stops you
    • Thirteen kinds of treats, from kibble and a rare golden fish up to milk tankers, fish markets and a mountain of yarn. Bigger ones are worth far more
    • The city is edible too: cones, lampposts, benches, bushes, pigeons and mice, then cars, vans, buses, statues and whole buildings. Watch your cat's mouth open wide and slurp them in
    • A dashed ring on a treat means you are too small for it, and the number shows the size you need
    • To absorb a cat or a treat you must be at least 1.2× its size. You grow by its size
    • Start on treats, then grow big enough to swallow bins, trees, cars, fountains and finally whole buildings. Anything with a glowing dotted outline is small enough for you to eat
    • Solo: Grow Rush has no rival cats. Race the clock to size 1000 on today's city, the same layout for everyone that day. Earn bronze, silver or gold, and copy your result to share
    • The whole island city is open. When only 5 cats are left, a final circle slowly closes in
    • Music and sound effects each have their own on/off switch and a volume slider in Settings. Menu music starts after your first tap. On iPhone, the ring/silent switch may also mute it
    • Small cats turn and accelerate faster, so dodge rather than run. Big cats dash further and pull small items in
    • Arrows at the screen edge point to nearby cats off-screen: an arrow with ! can eat you, a plain arrow you can eat. In Grow Rush a diamond points to a big tasty target. Colors can be changed in Settings for color vision
    • Eat things in quick succession to build a combo of up to ×1.5 extra growth
    • Every match starts with a 3-second countdown. In King of the Hill, rivals leave you alone for the first 10 seconds (a blue ring shows you're protected)
    • King of the Hill rivals have personalities: foragers, hunters, cowards and one nemesis who hunts you. Choose Easy, Normal or Hard in Settings
    • Power-ups (can be switched off in Settings): catnip for speed, a shield so nothing can eat you, and a magnet that pulls food in
    • Matches can be rainy, snowy or foggy, and the day turns to night as you play. Street lights come on in the dark
    • Tap the pause button at the top (or press P). The game also pauses itself if you switch apps
    • The last cat standing swells to fill the whole city… and then the whole of Cat Earth
    • Unlock new cat colors by playing, absorbing cats and winning
